For our 36th edition of Crypto In Montreal, we present the « **Bitcoin Decentralized Governance** ». It's a high-level overview of how the Bitcoin Blockchain works. It will discuss who are the participants of the Bitcoin network and how the decentralized governance model is achieved.

If you ever wondered how the bitcoin ecosystem works, what makes Bitcoin different from other cryptos or even why there are firm Bitcoin believers, then this presentation is for you.

Tech companies are announcing their crypto to compete with banks. During this time, while governments and financial institutions plan to introduce national cryptocurrencies, it is obvious the crypto market is only becoming more complex.

### Agenda

Understanding Bitcoin will allow you to make sense of the activities happening in this space. I will cover:

- Bitcoin cryptocurrency
- Wallets
- Blockchain
- Bitcoin S/W Protocol
- Nodes
- Miners
- Developers
- Users

### About our speaker (bio)

Since 2017, **Uzma Abbas** has been providing personalized training on all aspects of the cryptocurrency domain for new investors and enthusiasts wanting to enter the market. She works with crypto community leaders & Montreal Bitcoin Meetups to educate the public through her presentational series Bitcoin 101.
