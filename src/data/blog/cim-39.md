### Agenda

Money is the ultimate driver of human behaviour, yet its properties and how it is created is least understood. Fortunately, Bitcoin has sparked an awareness movement about what money is and how it’s linked to our economic realities.

In this 1-hour this presentation, you will learn about hard/sound money and its economic impacts. It will provide an understanding of Money that you will not get in a classroom. The presentation will include the following topics:

-Quick history of money
-Properties of sound/hard money
-Comparison of Fiat vs. Gold vs Bitcoin standards
