For the 35th edition of Crypto In Montreal, **Justin D. Harris** will present some discoveries he made regarding deep learning applications for NLU at Microsoft Research.

Our work focuses on providing free to use for **prediction** machine learning models stored in **smart contracts**. We define several incentive mechanisms to encourage **high quality data contributions** to update these shared models.

### About the speaker (bio)

Justin D. Harris has experience in various NLU (Natural Language Understanding) fields such as ontological representations, classification, entity tagging, dialog, and implementing neural networks for production systems. His main experience is in developing virtual personal assistants at Maluuba and deep learning applications for NLU at Microsoft Research. Find our more about Justin at <https://aka.ms/hodl>
